Terms of Service

1. The service

Echoreply lets you link your WhatsApp account so that an AI writes automatic replies in your texting style. You decide when it is on, and you can pause or disconnect at any time.

2. Your responsibilities

  • You may only connect a WhatsApp account that you own or are authorised to use.
  • You are responsible for the messages sent from your account, including automatic replies, and for following the laws that apply to you.
  • You must not use the service for spam, harassment, fraud, impersonation to deceive, or any unlawful purpose.

3. WhatsApp risk

Echoreply connects in the same way as WhatsApp Web and is not affiliated with or endorsed by WhatsApp or Meta. WhatsApp may limit or ban accounts that use automation. You use the service at your own risk and we are not liable for any restriction placed on your number.

4. Plans and payment

The Free plan has a daily reply limit. Premium is paid by bank transfer, is activated after we confirm your payment, and lasts for the period shown at checkout. Prices can change for future payments. Payments are non-refundable unless the law says otherwise.

5. Suspension

We may suspend or ban accounts that break these terms or put the service at risk. A suspended account is disconnected from WhatsApp immediately.

6. No guarantees

AI replies can be wrong or inappropriate. Review how it behaves, keep the automated-reply notice on where the law requires it, and do not rely on it for important, financial, legal or safety-related conversations. The service is provided "as is".

Privacy Policy

What we collect

  • Account data: name, email, hashed password, plan and payment references.
  • WhatsApp session: a login session stored on our server so your account stays linked.
  • Where data is kept: account and settings data are stored with Google Firebase (Authentication and Firestore).
  • Style samples: up to 100 short messages you wrote, saved to match your voice.
  • Consent record: the date, version, IP address and browser of your consent.
  • Usage counts: the number of replies sent per day. We do not store the content of conversations.

How messages are processed

When someone messages you, the message text, recent messages in that chat and your style samples are sent to our AI provider (Anthropic) to write a reply, then discarded by us. Please check the provider's own privacy terms.

Contacts' rights

Anyone who receives an automatic reply can send STOP to opt out. We store only a one-way hash of the contact's identifier for this purpose, not their phone number.

Your rights

You can withdraw consent (which disconnects WhatsApp and deletes the session and style samples) or delete your account from the dashboard at any time. For other requests, contact us at the email shown on the site.
