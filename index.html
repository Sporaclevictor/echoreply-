Set up in about two minutes

  1. Say yes, in writing

    Read what we access and tick each consent box. Your choice is recorded, and you can withdraw it later.

  2. Scan one QR code

    Link your WhatsApp from your phone, the same way you link WhatsApp Web.

  3. We learn your style

    Echoreply reads messages you wrote to pick up how you text, then drafts replies in that voice.

  4. Pause whenever

    Turn replies off, disconnect, or delete everything from your dashboard.

Built for consent

People who message you are told a reply is automated, and anyone can send STOP to opt out. We don't store your conversations: only a count of replies is kept so plan limits work.
